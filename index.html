Benchmarking Attention-Enhanced Encoder–Decoder Models for Polyp Segmentation in Colonoscopy Images

Accepted for Presentation • IEEE RAAICON 2026

This research systematically benchmarks attention-enhanced encoder–decoder deep architectures for semantic segmentation of colorectal polyps in colonoscopy imagery. The investigation focuses on enhancing boundary delineation accuracy for subtle, flat, and small polyps while maintaining low computational latency suitable for real-time clinical endoscopy decision support.

Domain: Biomedical Computer Vision • Semantic Segmentation

Department of Computer Science & Engineering • Daffodil International University
